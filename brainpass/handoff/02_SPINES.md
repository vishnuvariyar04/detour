# Topic spines for bands B and D

From **The Nupo Ladder**, the original curriculum plan. This is the *source of
topics*, not the structure to build.

## Read this before using the tables

The Ladder plans a year per band as **12 places × 4 weeks × 4 skills** — 192
skills, on an eight-strand map with spaced review and a weekly parent report.
None of that is built. What is built is a different, smaller shape:

| | The Ladder plans | What actually ships |
|---|---|---|
| Unit of content | a place, a week, a skill | a section, a unit, a stop |
| Per band | 12 places, 48 weeks, 192 skills | 4 sections, 12 units, 48 stops |
| Questions | generated per skill | 324, authored by hand |
| Progress | Leitner review, 1·3·7·16·35 days | a linear ladder, stop 1 to stop 48 |

**Build the shipped shape.** Take the topics below and fold them into 4 sections
× 3 units × 4 stops, as proposed in `01_BUILD_GUIDE.md` §4.1. Do not try to
build the Ladder — it is a new schema, a new roadmap and a spaced-repetition
engine, and it is not this job.

The chosen themes pull mainly from one place each:

- **Band B → Puzzles & Logic**, from Pattern Lab (place 04)
- **Band D → Reasoning**, from Pattern Lab (place 04)

Everything else below is context: it tells you what a child of that age is
expected to know, which is what keeps a question pitched right.

## Two constraints the Ladder does not mention

**The count pad stops at 9.** Any answer above 9 cannot be tapped on the number
pad, and the checker rejects it as an answer a child cannot physically give. The
nth term, binary values, squares and roots all want bigger numbers — pose them
as multiple choice, or agree on a new input before authoring.

**Everything must work in about six seconds, silently, over another app.** That
was the original filter for the eight strands and it still applies. A question
needing a paragraph of reading, or sound, is the wrong question here — which is
why the two shipped skills are almost entirely pictures.
