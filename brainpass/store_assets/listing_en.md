## App name (30)
Nupo: Kids Earn Screen Time

## Short description (80)
A short lesson before YouTube and games. Screen time that teaches, ages 5-12.

## Full description (4000)
Screen time that teaches. Nupo steps in before the apps your child already loves, like YouTube and games, with a short lesson first. They learn a little, earn their play time, and when the daily limit is reached, it's done for today.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Your child opens YouTube or a game you picked.
2. Nupo opens first with a short lesson from their own course.
3. They finish it and the app opens, with a countdown on top.
4. When the daily limit is reached, the owl says "See you tomorrow."

No more arguing over the tablet. The learning happens in the minutes they were going to spend on their phone anyway.

A REAL COURSE, NOT RANDOM QUIZZES
Every lesson is a step in a course written for your child's age. Each course has 48 lessons, from the first idea to the boss level at the end.
- Ages 5-6: Number Sense. Counting, ten-frames, comparing, shapes and parts of a whole.
- Ages 7-8: Puzzles & Logic. Missing numbers, story sums, order and position, codes and clocks.
- Ages 9-10: Think Like a Coder. Reading programs, loops, conditions and finding bugs.
- Ages 11-12: Reasoning. Number patterns, logic grids, binary, codes and 3D shapes.

HOW EACH LESSON TEACHES
- A new idea first, shown with a picture.
- Then questions on exactly that idea.
- Stuck? A hint that nudges, never just the answer.
- Got one wrong? It comes back at the end, until it's answered right.
- A boss question finishes every unit.

YOU SET THE RULES
- Choose which apps need a lesson first.
- Decide how much play time each lesson earns, from 5 to 60 minutes.
- Set a daily limit for each app.
- A parent PIN protects every setting, and you can pause Nupo any time.

SEE THEIR PROGRESS
The Learning tab shows where your child is on their course, what's up next, their streak, how many questions they answered today and this week, and how many they got right.

MADE FOR KIDS, BUILT FOR PARENTS
- No ads inside Nupo, ever.
- Your child never creates an account. You sign in, they just learn.
- Their answers stay on the phone.
- Lessons are short, friendly and led by Nupo the owl.

NUPO PRO
Nupo Pro is an optional subscription with a free trial for new subscribers. You can manage or cancel it any time in Google Play.

SETUP
To show a lesson when a chosen app opens, Nupo needs two permissions you turn on during setup: Usage access (to notice which app was opened) and Display over other apps (to show the lesson). Nupo only acts on the apps you picked.
