# Nupo — App Store Listing & Copy Bank

Repositioned around **learning** (July 2026). Nupo is a daily learning habit for
kids 5–11, delivered through the phone time they already have. Do **not** use
"screen time", "block apps", "reduce screen time" or "parental control" anywhere.

---

## App store copy

**App title** (Play, ≤30 chars)
> Nupo: Daily Learning for Kids

**Subtitle / tagline** (≤30 chars)
> Learning they'll actually do.

**Short description** (≤80 chars)
> The daily learning habit for kids 5–11 — built into the games they love.

**Full description**

> **Finally, learning they won't fight you on.**
>
> You bought the workbooks. You tried the flashcards. You downloaded the
> "educational" apps that got opened once. Every parent knows the real problem:
> it isn't finding good learning — it's getting your kid to actually sit down
> and do it.
>
> Nupo fixes that with one simple idea. Kids never quit the one habit you wish
> they would — reaching for the phone. So Nupo puts a tiny, friendly learning
> moment right there: a couple of quick questions before the game or video they
> were already headed for. They answer, they get a little sharper, and then they
> play. No nagging. No battles. No "five more minutes."
>
> It becomes a daily habit because it rides on a habit they already have.
>
> **How Nupo works**
> • Your child reaches for a game or video they love.
> • Nupo pops up with a short, playful learning moment — a few questions at just
>   the right level.
> • They answer, and off they go to play.
> • You choose which apps spark a lesson, how many questions, and how long they
>   play.
>
> **Real learning, ages 5–11**
> • Questions adapt to your child's age — counting and simple sums for little
>   ones; mental math and general knowledge for older kids; logic puzzles for the
>   ready-for-more.
> • Bite-sized by design. A few minutes at a time is what actually sticks.
> • Warm and encouraging, never punishing. A wrong answer gently shows the way —
>   no lost stars, no shame.
>
> **Made for parents**
> • Set it up once, in minutes.
> • A private PIN keeps every setting yours.
> • See at a glance what your child practiced today.
> • Change the apps, questions and minutes whenever you like.
>
> Nupo isn't about taking the phone away. It's about turning the time they're
> already spending into a few minutes of real learning — every single day. The
> kind of small, steady habit that quietly adds up.
>
> Give your kid a little sharper, every day. Download Nupo.

**Tagline options**
